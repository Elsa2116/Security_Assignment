# SecureFlow Acceptable Use Policy (Local Project Draft)

Users must use SecureFlow only for authorized organizational requests and approvals. Users must protect passwords, MFA devices, recovery codes, and sessions; must not share accounts; and must report suspected compromise, phishing, or unauthorized access immediately.

Administrators may review security and approval records for legitimate security, audit, and operational purposes. Administrators must not approve their own privileged role changes or bypass required approval separation.

Requests must be accurate, lawful, and limited to the business purpose described. Uploading secrets, malware, unnecessary personal data, or unlawful content is prohibited.

The organization may suspend accounts, revoke sessions, preserve evidence, and investigate suspected misuse. Replace this draft with the university-approved policy before production deployment.
