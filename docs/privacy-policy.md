# SecureFlow Privacy Policy (Local Project Draft)

## Data collected

SecureFlow stores account name, email address, organization membership, roles, request details, security events, session metadata, and audit records required to operate approval workflows.

## Purpose and access

Data is used for identity verification, access control, request approval, security monitoring, and auditability. Access is restricted by organization and role. Passwords are never stored in plaintext; credential passwords use bcrypt through Better Auth.

## Retention

Organizations should define retention periods for requests, audit events, sessions, backups, and security incidents based on institutional and legal requirements. Deletion requests must preserve records required for security, fraud prevention, and legal obligations.

## Sharing and transfers

Production email delivery, CAPTCHA verification, breach-password checking, object storage, and SIEM services may process limited technical data. Production operators must document each provider, region, retention period, and data-processing agreement before activation.

## User rights and contact

The organization administrator is responsible for access requests, correction requests, retention decisions, and privacy incidents. Replace this draft with the university-approved contact and legal notice before production deployment.
